🆘 Offline Landslide Safety Guide

This information is available without internet access.

⚠️ Before a Landslide

Monitor rainfall and official warnings. Keep basic emergency supplies ready and avoid unnecessary travel near vulnerable slopes during severe weather.

🚨 During an Emergency

Move away from unstable slopes and follow instructions from local authorities and emergency services.

🏠 After a Landslide

Do not return to affected areas until authorities say it is safe. Watch for further ground movement and hazards.

🎒 Emergency Preparedness

Keep drinking water, basic first-aid supplies, a flashlight and important contact information ready.
